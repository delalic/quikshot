1.0 Current June 3, 2026

QuikShot is live on the App Store.

The first public release of QuikShot: a private, iPhone-first tracker for peptides, GLP-1s, oral routines, supplies, reminders, and dose history.

New
  • Today. See what is due, what is overdue, what was logged today, the next suggested injection site, and the supply context for the next dose.
  • Plan. Build flexible protocols with one-time, daily, weekly, fixed-interval, multi-time, and phase-based schedules.
  • Calendar and history. Review upcoming doses, logged doses, skipped doses, missed doses, and past corrections by date.
  • Compound library. Start from common compounds, supported route/unit defaults, familiar aliases and brand names, or your own custom compounds.
  • Injection and oral logging. Track injections, capsules, tablets, and liquids in one daily flow.
  • Reminders. Use privacy-safe notification defaults, optional detailed reminder copy, snooze, grouped simultaneous reminders, and Mark taken for detailed single-dose notifications.
  • Site rotation. Keep injection-site history and suggestions close to the dose that needs them.
  • Supplies. Track inventory, supply forecasts, reconstitution profiles, draw/IU calculations, prepared syringes, and injection prep.
  • Combined injection prep. Prepare compatible injections together when that matches the real routine.
  • Estimated levels. View per-compound estimates from your logged history, with editable half-life inputs where useful.
  • Private by default. No required account, no ads, no third-party analytics, optional iCloud sync, and JSON backup/restore.
Improved
  • Calm daily flow. QuikShot keeps high-signal details visible while keeping deeper setup and correction tools out of the way until they are needed.
  • User-controlled data. Data starts local, iCloud sync is optional, and JSON backup/restore gives you another way to keep a copy.
  • Plain-language safety boundaries. The app performs arithmetic on values you enter; it does not recommend doses or replace clinical judgment.
